Convertopia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Convertopia is a file and unit converter for Android made by Imi M. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

Your files stay on your phone

Every conversion (video, audio, images, PDF, documents, data formats, units, QR codes) happens on your device. Your files are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Converted files are saved only to the folder you choose on your phone (Download/Convertopia unless you pick another one).

What we collect

Nothing. Convertopia has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code or barcode. The image is processed on your phone to read the code and is not saved or sent anywhere. You can say no to camera access and still use every other feature, including reading a QR code from a picture.

Photos and files

The app only sees the files and photos you pick yourself with Android's photo picker or file picker, or share to it from another app. It does not scan or read anything else on your phone.

Internet

Internet is used for one thing only: downloading the latest currency exchange rates from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API) when you open the Currency converter. This request contains no personal information and none of your files. Like any website, that service can see the IP address of the request. The rates are saved on your phone so currency conversion also works offline. Links you tap in the app (for example the Open source licenses page) open in your browser.

Notifications

A notification is shown only while a long conversion is running, so it can finish in the background. It is not used for anything else.

Children

Convertopia does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
